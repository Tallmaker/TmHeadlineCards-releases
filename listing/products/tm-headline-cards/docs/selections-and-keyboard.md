# Selections and keyboard

- Click a card name to select its category.
- Click a point on a trend line to select that category and period.
- The headline and the change do not select.

## Keyboard

- Press **Tab** to reach a card name, then **Enter** or **Space** to select its category.
- Press **Tab** to reach a trend line. Focus starts on the latest point.
- Press the arrow keys to move along the points, and **Enter** or **Space** to select the focused point.

Each card name and point has a label for screen readers with the category, period and value.
