# Selections and keyboard

- Click a card name to select its category.
- Click a point on a trend line to select that category and period.
- Drag across several points of one card, with a mouse, a pen or a finger, to select a range of periods. A range replaces the earlier selection.
- The headline and the change do not select.

## Keyboard

- Press **Tab** to reach a card name, then **Enter** or **Space** to select its category.
- Press **Tab** to reach a trend line. Focus starts on the latest point.
- Press the arrow keys to move along the points, and **Enter** or **Space** to select the focused point.
- Hold **Shift** and press an arrow key to extend a range of periods from the point where you began.

Nothing is selected while you edit the sheet or in a snapshot.

Each card name and point has a label for screen readers with the category, period and value.

## Tooltips and sheet links

Hover a trend point, or move to it with the arrow keys, to see its month, value, comparison and change. A shortened headline shows its full number the same way. Turn tooltips off under **Appearance → Tooltip**.

When **Appearance → Link** names a sheet, each card has an **Open sheet ›** link in its footer that opens that sheet. The link is hidden while you edit the sheet.
