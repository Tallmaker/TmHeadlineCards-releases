# Choose a look

Four starting points. Each one names the options to set. Everything else keeps its default.

## Operations status board

A wall screen where red must jump out, such as support resolution times against an SLA.

- **Comparison**: Good direction **Lower is better**, a Neutral band of 5, Change as **Percent change**, and a label such as `vs SLA`.
- **Status colour**: turn on **Card background** and **Trend**.
- **Trend**: Reference line **Comparison**, so the target shows as a dashed line.
- **Styling → Cards**: a corner radius of about 10.

## Executive summary

A few large numbers with their story, on a slide-like sheet.

- **Layout**: Card arrangement **Stacked**, Alignment **Center**, Columns **At most 3**.
- **Data**: add a **Headline** measure, such as `Sum(TOTAL <Category> Sales)` for the year to date, and a **Comparison 2** for a stretch target.
- **Trend**: **Area** or **Line** with the latest, highest and lowest points marked.
- **Styling → Cards**: **Filled**, with **Spacious** padding.

## Dense store or region grid

Up to 24 categories on one object, for scanning.

- **Layout**: Card arrangement **Compact**, Card order **Change, highest first**.
- **Comparison**: turn off **Show change label** to save space.
- **Trend**: **Line** with Markers **Latest point**.
- **Styling → Cards**: **Plain**, so the gap alone separates the cards.

## Monthly scorecard

A row of wide cards next to a table or a filter pane.

- **Layout**: Card arrangement **Side by side**, Columns **At most 1** or **At most 2**.
- **Trend**: **Win and loss**, with Latest points shown set to 12, for a month-by-month record against plan.
- **Status colour**: **Card accent bar**.
- **Styling → Cards**: **Outlined**, with the accent bar on the left.
