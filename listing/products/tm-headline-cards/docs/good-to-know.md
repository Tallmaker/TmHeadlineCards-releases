# Good to know

Tm Headline Cards needs a Time dimension and one Value measure. A Category dimension is optional: with one, each category gets a card and the object uses up to four measures; without one, the object draws one card for each Value measure, up to eight, and a Comparison measure belongs to the Value before it. At most 24 cards show, with a note when there are more.

An Auto-format measure follows the decimal and thousand separators of the app, and changes show at most two decimals. When the Value is a percentage, its change is in percentage points, for example +2.1 pp.

On a small object the cards first drop the change label and the indicator, which need width, then the trend, then the change. The headline stays: it shrinks, then switches to the app's number abbreviation, such as 1.2G, rather than being cut off.

Charts made with 0.1 keep their change display when you upgrade. Their cards become plain, without the fixed border; choose **Outlined** in the Styling panel to bring a border back.

Story snapshots keep the data as saved.

Image export, PDF export and sharing are not available for this chart. Data export and View data are available.
