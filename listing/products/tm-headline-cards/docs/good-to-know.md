# Good to know

Tm Headline Cards needs two dimensions, Category and Time, and one or two measures.

On a small object the cards first drop the change label and the indicator, which need width, then the trend, then the change. The headline stays: it shrinks, then switches to the app's number abbreviation, such as 1.2G, rather than being cut off.

Charts made with 0.1 keep their change display when you upgrade. Their cards become plain, without the fixed border; choose **Outlined** in the Styling panel to bring a border back.

Story snapshots keep the data as saved.

Image export, PDF export and sharing are not available for this chart. Data export and View data are available.
