# Good to know

Tm Headline Cards needs two dimensions, Category and Time, and one or two measures.

On a small object the cards drop the trend line first, then the change. The headline stays: it shrinks, then switches to the app's number abbreviation, such as 1.2G, rather than being cut off.

Story snapshots keep the data as saved.

Image export, PDF export and sharing are not available for this chart. Data export and View data are available.
