# Property panel

## Data

- **Category** (dimension): one card per value.
- **Time** (dimension): the order of the trend line. The chart sorts it newest first to read the latest values, and draws the line oldest to newest.
- **Value** (measure): the headline and the trend line.
- **Comparison** (measure, optional): the change shown under the headline.

The cards follow the Category order in the latest period.

## Styling

- **Comparison display**: **Absolute difference** shows Value minus Comparison in the Value format. **Percent change** shows the change as a percent of the Comparison. When the Comparison is zero, the card shows a dash instead of a percent.
- **Background color**, **Font color**, **Font** and **Font size** override the app theme.
- **Header color** sets the card names and the trend line.
- **Grid color** sets the card borders.
